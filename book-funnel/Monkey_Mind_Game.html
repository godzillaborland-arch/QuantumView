How it works

Your mind is full of monkeys, thoughts leaping in from every direction. You're the one at the center, sitting still. Mostly.

  • Breathe in: press and hold anywhere (or hold Space). The ring fills.
  • Breathe out: let go. A wave rolls out over the out-breath and settles every monkey it reaches.
  • A full breath reaches the whole ring. A quick jab barely leaves your head. (Ask me how I know.)
  • If one gets through, things go foggy for a bit. Your next full breath brings you back. That's a return, and returns are the whole score.

Amber, round tag: everyday chatter. Worry, to-do, scroll, lane drift.
Crimson, diamond tag: the classics, in Sanskrit. Let one go to see what it means.

Sits end with a bell, not a game over. P or Esc pauses; so does switching tabs.
