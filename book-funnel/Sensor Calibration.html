Certain Arrival at Self-Realization

Your role: You are the Maintenance Engineer. The vehicle drives itself on Self-Drive. You only tend its four sensors.

Each cycle: Draw turbulence, then play one Engineering Spec or continue without one. Correct a sensor that has gone offline, or Cultivate a healthy one so the next turbulence against it passes.

Self-Drive: With 1 or fewer sensors offline, Self-Drive stays engaged and the vehicle advances one space per cycle. With 2 or more offline it disengages. Three stationary cycles in a row ends the route.

The road ahead: The next turbulence is always visible. From cycle 8, two arrive each cycle. Dhyāna (three times per journey) lets the next turbulence pass in stillness.

Lifetime Charge: Energy lapses drain it. A cycle with all four sensors clear restores 5 through Kripa.
