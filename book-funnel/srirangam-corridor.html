The Srirangam Corridor

Training wheels to stillness

Your attention is an elephant in a busy market. The trunk grabs at everything, and yelling at it doesn't help. So you hand it a bamboo stick to hold, walk it through seven temple gates, and watch the training wheels come off.

Warm-up · the salary count

Count your pay

Twelve notes. Fake money, real attention. Tap each note once as it lands.

Tap the note.Click the note or press Space.

Twelve for twelve. Nobody's mind wanders while counting their own pay. Attention isn't broken; it goes where the value is.

So give this walk some value. The gold ring at the top is the value you're giving this moment. It grows each time you come back.

Gate 4 · the ten-minute contract

Your worries followed you in

Work pings, Mom’s messages, the milk running out. They'll keep flying at you. So make them a deal: they wait on a shelf for ten minutes, and in minute eleven you get every one of them back, untouched.
