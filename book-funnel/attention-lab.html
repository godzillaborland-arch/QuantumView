01 / Diagnose

Diagnostics

See the reactive loop.

Reactive friction / 2D≈ 2 min

Monkey Mind

Enter the willpower trap. Meet a reactive system in which pushing harder can create more friction, and notice the impulse to answer every disturbance.

The inquiry

Does every disturbance require intervention—or can the correction itself keep the loop alive?

Launch Monkey Mind↗
After the experiment

Recall one moment when your urge to control increased. What happened immediately before it?

02 / Stabilize

Controls

Explore shielding and the cost of correction.

Sensory shielding10 min

The Srirangam Corridor

Explore the elephant-stick metaphor: give attention a simple anchor as sensory invitations pass by. Enter a ten-minute contract with one practice.

The inquiry

Can a chosen anchor reduce the need to renegotiate attention with every passing stimulus?

Enter the corridor↗
After the experiment

What changed when you committed to an anchor? Distinguish steadiness from strain.

Manual → Autonomous≈ 3 min

The Freeway Navigator

Compare the demands of a manual clutch with an FSD-inspired regenerative glide. Investigate habitual correction and the possibility of less effort.

The inquiry

What changes when continual correction gives way to a steadier mode of navigation?

Launch the navigator↗
After the experiment

Where did effort help? Where did it become repetitive? Notice the difference.

03 / Discern

Logic

Investigate the assumptions behind the practice.

Assumption debugger / 3D≈ 3 min

Neti NetiThe Debugger Run

Move through a fast-paced logic runner that brings meditation assumptions into view. Practice “not this, not this” as a prompt for inquiry.

The inquiry

Can you recognize an idea about meditation without making that idea the measure of your experience?

Run the debugger↗
After the experiment

Which assumption was hardest to release? What made it feel necessary?

04 / Open

Horizon

From experiential qualities to the silent canvas.

APB avionics / 3D≈ 2 min

The APB CompassFlight of the Mind

Pilot a glider through floating thoughts while balancing Absorption, Peace, and Bliss. Explore the relationship between direction, disturbance, and inner qualities.

The inquiry

Can you notice absorption, peace, and bliss without turning them into another set of targets to chase?

Launch the glider↗
After the experiment

Did you start pursuing a particular quality? How did that pursuit affect your attention?

Unconditioned presenceOpen-ended

Aether

Arrive at the silent canvas. An ambient space invites you to explore presence without another problem to solve. Stay for a moment, or longer.

The inquiry

What remains when there is no next correction, explanation, or achievement to pursue?

Enter Aether↗
A gentle invitation

Notice the urge to make something happen. Let that urge, too, appear within the space.
