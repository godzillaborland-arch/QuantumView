## Retained, adapted, and deliberately excluded
The redesign preserves the original’s narrative topics, not every sentence verbatim. The shorter opening, endorsement sections, FAQ, and grouped navigation are additions from the redesign. The full Global Voices collection remains available through its link rather than every quote appearing inline.

The automatic arrival popup and “No thanks, I’ll find it later” dismissal were intentionally replaced with a click-to-open trailer popup, as agreed. The concept cover was replaced with the user-supplied real cover.

Absolute spiritual assertions are framed as the author’s experience or the tradition’s teaching. Historical anecdotes, supernatural claims, the mentor’s attainment, and claims about spiritual continuity after death have not been independently established by this review. “The Proof” is retained as the original book part title, not represented as scientific validation.

## Still requires source material or operational confirmation
- No actual manuscript sample has been supplied; the page offers an overview and a discussion.
- No automated receipt verification, email delivery, or protected Lab login has been implemented in this static landing page. The page explains the existing email-based redemption process; the Lab remains locked.
- Eight Lab experiences and the 30-day guide are advertised on the original page; their protected contents were not inspected or counted.
- Amazon’s exact product URL and bonus eligibility need confirmation before promising Amazon redemption.
- Publication timing is copied from the current original page, not independently confirmed with the publisher.
- Retail availability, checkout, bonus redemption, and video playback through to completion were not transaction-tested.
