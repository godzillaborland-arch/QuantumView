How to play

From effort to effortless

  1. Steering

    Computer: arrow keys or A / D. Phone: swipe left or right from the middle of the screen, tap the left or right half, or use the ◀ ▶ buttons.

  2. Awareness Pulse

    Press Spacebar or tap the ◎ icon to instantly clear all immediate distractions. It takes time to recharge.

  3. Dodge the vṛttis

    Distractions escalate as you drive. Hitting them costs a sensor. Each correct gate cleans one sensor.

  4. Three stages of the drive

    Manual mode (0–2 correct). Lane assist (3–5 correct). Full Self-Driving (6+ correct): the car steers around the vṛttis by itself.

  5. Scoring & Goal

    Ten correct gates reach Dhyāna. After that, the road stays open in endless mode with contemplative reflections.
