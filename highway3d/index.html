Highway to Dhyāna

You start in manual mode, steering through a noisy road of vṛttis. Use your Awareness Pulse (Spacebar or ◎) to clear immediate distractions when the mind gets crowded. Steer through the toll gate with the right answer to clear your sensors and let the car take over.

How to play

From effort to effortless

  1. Steering and speed

    Computer: ← → or A/D steer, ↑ ↓ or W/S drive faster or slower. Phone: tap either side or swipe left/right to steer, swipe up or down to change speed, or use the ◀ ▶ buttons. Faster earns more points; slower gives you more time to read.

  2. Awareness Pulse

    Press Spacebar or tap ◎ to vaporize nearby vṛttis into golden light. It recharges in ten seconds.

  3. Windshield clarity

    Every vṛtti you hit muddies a sensor. Correct answers trigger a clean golden wiper sweep.

  4. Three stages & the APB Compass

    Manual mode (0–2 correct). Lane assist (3–5 correct). FSD (6+ correct): the car steers itself while the tanpura fifth swells.

  5. Campaign

    Three chapters, one per part of the book, each with its own road and hazards: drifting worries (watch the arrow), mud puddles, and mirages that FSD cannot see. Clear the gates, then beat the boss by winning two of its three crimson gates. Fewer wrong gates earn more stars.

  6. 2 players

    Split screen on one keyboard (P1: A/D and W, P2: ←/→ and ↑) or two gamepads. Same road, same gates: whoever clears more reaches Dhyāna first.

  7. Free drive

    Ten correct gates reach Dhyāna. After that, reflection portals keep the road open forever.
