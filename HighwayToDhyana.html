How to play

Keep Full Self-Driving engaged

  1. The goal

    Clear ten toll gates to reach Dhyāna. After that the road stays open in endless mode, faster each lap.

  2. Steering

    Computer: arrow keys or A / D. Phone: swipe left or right from the middle of the screen, tap the left or right half, or use the ◀ ▶ buttons. Each move shifts one lane.

  3. Dodge the vṛttis

    Worry, Craving, Doubt, New Email and their friends come down the lanes. Each one you hit puts mud on the lens and costs a sensor. Lose all three and the autopilot disengages.

  4. Toll gates

    A question from the book appears at the top, with the three answers labeled Left, Center and Right. A golden gate follows. Steer into the lane with the right answer. A wrong gate drops you into manual mode and ends the run, and the end screen shows the right answer.

  5. Sensors and Satya

    Every correct answer cleans one sensor, the way truthfulness clears the lens.

  6. Scoring

    Points build with distance. Each correct gate adds 100 × your streak. Hitting a vṛtti resets the streak.

  7. The road

    It begins as a noisy city of billboards and static. With each right answer it grows quieter, until only gold lane lines and stars remain.

Tips: tapping is quicker than swiping at high speed. On iPhone, start swipes away from the screen edge, and turn off silent mode to hear the sound.
